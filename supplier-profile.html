Business Profile

How DSCG sourcing works

DSCG builds and supports business systems, websites, software products, brand assets, operational projects, and specialized client engagements. Procurement and supplier sourcing are additional ways we support those engagements. The sourcing fields shown on this page reflect current client-directed requests. For each request, DSCG defines the relevant requirements, conducts targeted supplier outreach, compares available options, evaluates fit, and develops a shortlist or recommendation for the client. Depending on the engagement, a requirement may involve finished products, materials or inputs, commercial supplies, equipment, components, custom manufacturing, private-label or OEM capabilities, or other products and supplier services relevant to the client's objective. DSCG acts as a sourcing and evaluation partner rather than a stocking retailer; standing inventory is not required for these engagements.

01 · CLIENT NEED

A requirement starts the search

Each current sourcing engagement begins with an actual client need: use case, specifications, quantity, budget, fulfillment expectations, customization, production, or other project-specific requirements.

02 · TARGETED OUTREACH

We identify and contact potential suppliers

DSCG reaches out to manufacturers and suppliers whose products, production capabilities, or commercial terms appear relevant to the active client request rather than building an unrelated standing assortment.

03 · EVALUATION

Products and suppliers are compared

Evaluation can include quality, specifications, pricing, MOQs, lead times, customization, manufacturing capability, fulfillment, packaging, communication, documentation, and the supplier's ability to support the client's intended use.

04 · CLIENT RECOMMENDATION

Suitable options go back to the client

DSCG organizes the relevant findings and presents appropriate supplier, manufacturer, or product options to the client so the client can make an informed procurement decision.

Current Client-Directed Sourcing

Active sourcing fields

DSCG is currently conducting supplier and manufacturer searches across the broad fields below in response to active client requests. The examples are representative rather than exhaustive. Current briefs may involve finished products, ingredients or components, commercial equipment, supplies, customized products, private-label or OEM production, or adjacent supplier capabilities within the same field. Searches may include U.S. or international manufacturers where appropriate to the engagement. Across every field, the process remains the same: client requirement, targeted outreach, evaluation, comparison, and recommendation back to the client.

01 · FOOD & BEVERAGE

Food, beverage & related manufacturing

Current client requests in this field span the broader supply chain, from ingredients and production inputs through finished consumer or foodservice products and custom manufacturing. Each search is defined by the client's concept, specifications, volumes, price targets, production needs, and fulfillment requirements.

  • Finished, prepared, specialty, and foodservice products
  • Ingredients, inputs, bulk supply, and formulation-related needs
  • Wholesale, customized, private-label, OEM, and related manufacturing capabilities
02 · KITCHEN & FOODSERVICE

Commercial kitchen, service & foodservice systems

Current client requests in this field span front- and back-of-house products, supplies, smallwares, storage and service systems, sanitation products, and commercial equipment. Searches can range from simple manual items to powered preparation, temperature-control, cooking, beverage, bakery, or other specialized systems required by the client's operation.

  • Operational supplies, smallwares, tools, storage, transport, and service products
  • Commercial preparation, cooking, holding, refrigeration, sanitation, and beverage equipment
  • Specialized foodservice systems and adjacent equipment selected for the actual operation
03 · BUSINESS EQUIPMENT · HARDWARE · TOOLS

Operational, technical & business equipment

Current client requests in this field span general operational products and tools as well as electronic, connected, automation, facility, component, technical, and measurement-oriented equipment. Each search follows the client's intended use, required specifications, environment, integration needs, budget, and purchasing requirements.

  • General operational products, tools, hardware, and facility equipment
  • Electronic, connected, component, sensing, and automation-oriented products
  • Specialized technical and measurement equipment where relevant to the engagement
04 · PET PRODUCTS

Dog-focused products

Current client requests in this field are dog-focused and include, but are not limited to, treats. Those requests drive targeted outreach to dog-product manufacturers and suppliers for product and supplier evaluation.

  • Dog treats, consumables, supplies, and related dog-focused products
  • Products and supplier capabilities relevant to the client's stated requirements
  • Wholesale, customized, and other commercial supply options where applicable
Current project scope: dog products only. Cat products should not be included unless specifically requested in writing.

Sample & Shipping Policy

Samples support current client evaluations.

When physical review is relevant to a current client-directed sourcing engagement, DSCG may request a small representative sample set so product quality, packaging, specifications, and overall fit can be evaluated before options are presented to the client. Samples are requested for evaluation, not for accumulating promotional inventory.

Evaluation quantity

A small box or representative assortment is generally sufficient to assess the products relevant to the client's sourcing requirements.

International shipping

Before an international sample shipment is dispatched, DSCG requires written confirmation that both the sample products and shipping charges are complimentary.

Recipient contact

DSCG maintains a business receiving point in Charlotte, North Carolina. Recipient phone number and complete shipping details are provided directly to confirmed suppliers rather than published publicly.

Supplier Contact

Supplier outreach for active client needs

DSCG contacts manufacturers and suppliers because current client requirements have created defined sourcing needs. Depending on client confidentiality and the engagement, DSCG may communicate the relevant product, performance, production, commercial, or fulfillment requirements without publicly identifying the client. Suppliers responding to an outreach request may provide product information, sample availability, specifications, wholesale terms, MOQs, lead times, customization or manufacturing capabilities, relevant technical or commercial documentation, and fulfillment details so DSCG can evaluate fit for the engagement.
