Sample & Shipping Policy

Small samples. Clear terms.

DSCG uses supplier samples for practical evaluation, not for accumulating promotional inventory. A small representative assortment is preferred.

Evaluation quantity

A small box or representative assortment is generally sufficient for product, packaging, and supplier review.

International shipping

Before an international sample shipment is dispatched, DSCG requires written confirmation that both the sample products and shipping charges are complimentary.

Recipient contact

DSCG maintains a business receiving point in Charlotte, North Carolina. Recipient phone number and complete shipping details are provided directly to confirmed suppliers rather than published publicly.

Supplier Contact

Manufacturer or product-partner inquiry

Suppliers may send sample availability, wholesale terms, private-label details, MOQs, production lead times, and U.S. fulfillment information for review.
