Business Profile

How DSCG sourcing works

DSCG builds and supports business systems, websites, software products, brand assets, operational projects, and specialized client engagements. Supplier sourcing is one of the ways we support those engagements. When a client identifies a product need and requests sourcing assistance, DSCG defines the relevant requirements, conducts targeted supplier outreach, compares available options, evaluates fit, and develops a shortlist or recommendation for the client. We do not present ourselves as a traditional retailer maintaining standing inventory across these categories.

01 · CLIENT NEED

A requirement starts the search

A sourcing engagement begins with a client's actual need: the product, use case, quantity, budget, fulfillment expectations, customization needs, or other project-specific requirements.

02 · TARGETED OUTREACH

We identify and contact potential suppliers

DSCG reaches out to manufacturers and suppliers whose products or capabilities appear relevant to the client's request rather than building an unrelated standing assortment.

03 · EVALUATION

Products and suppliers are compared

Evaluation can include product quality, specifications, pricing, MOQs, lead times, customization, fulfillment, packaging, communication, and the supplier's ability to support the client's intended use.

04 · CLIENT RECOMMENDATION

Suitable options go back to the client

DSCG organizes the relevant findings and presents appropriate supplier or product options to the client so the client can make an informed procurement decision.

Client-Directed Sourcing Areas

Product categories we can source by request

The categories below describe areas in which DSCG can conduct supplier searches when a client engagement creates a relevant product need. They are not standing retail inventories or blanket claims that every listed product is being purchased at all times. The process is consistent across categories: client requirement, targeted outreach, supplier and product evaluation, then recommendation back to the client.

01 · FOOD & BEVERAGE

Food, beverage & culinary products

When a client requests food or beverage sourcing, DSCG can identify and evaluate suppliers for products that fit the client's concept, operating needs, specifications, quantities, price targets, and fulfillment requirements.

  • Packaged food and beverage products
  • Ingredients, sauces, seasonings, condiments, and pantry items
  • Snacks, specialty products, and foodservice-ready formats
  • Wholesale, bulk, private-label, and business-use requirements
02 · KITCHEN & FOODSERVICE

Commercial kitchen & foodservice supplies

For restaurant, catering, hospitality, or foodservice clients with a defined need, DSCG can source and compare products based on the actual operation, service model, capacity, workflow, budget, and project requirements.

  • Cookware, bakeware, utensils, knives, and prep tools
  • Smallwares, food storage, containers, and organization
  • Serviceware, tabletop, beverage-service, and catering products
  • Restaurant, hospitality, sanitation, and disposable supplies
03 · BUSINESS EQUIPMENT · HARDWARE · TOOLS

Operational equipment & tools

When a client needs equipment, tools, hardware, storage, maintenance products, or other practical operating supplies, DSCG can conduct targeted supplier outreach and compare options against the client's stated use and purchasing requirements.

  • Small business and operational equipment
  • Hand tools, measuring tools, hardware, and maintenance items
  • Storage, organization, worksite, and facility products
  • Technology accessories and practical business-use hardware
04 · PET PRODUCTS

Current client request: dog-focused products

A current client has requested DSCG's assistance sourcing products for dogs, including but not limited to treats. That request has led to targeted outreach to dog-product manufacturers and suppliers for product and supplier evaluation.

  • Dog treats and dog-focused consumables
  • Simple and single-ingredient treat formats
  • Dog supplies relevant to the client's sourcing requirements
  • Wholesale and supplier capabilities appropriate to the engagement
Current project scope: dog products only. Cat products should not be included unless specifically requested in writing.
Sample & Shipping Policy

Samples support active evaluation.

When physical review is relevant to a client-directed sourcing engagement, DSCG may request a small representative sample set so product quality, packaging, specifications, and overall fit can be evaluated before options are presented to the client. Samples are requested for evaluation, not for accumulating promotional inventory.

Evaluation quantity

A small box or representative assortment is generally sufficient to assess the products relevant to the client's sourcing requirements.

International shipping

Before an international sample shipment is dispatched, DSCG requires written confirmation that both the sample products and shipping charges are complimentary.

Recipient contact

DSCG maintains a business receiving point in Charlotte, North Carolina. Recipient phone number and complete shipping details are provided directly to confirmed suppliers rather than published publicly.

Supplier Contact

Supplier outreach tied to a sourcing need

DSCG typically contacts manufacturers and suppliers because an active client requirement has created a specific sourcing need. Suppliers responding to an outreach request may provide relevant product information, sample availability, wholesale terms, MOQs, lead times, customization capabilities, and U.S. fulfillment details so DSCG can evaluate fit for the engagement.
